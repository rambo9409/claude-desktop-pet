# Motion sprite sheets

Built-in imagegen, one request per character. Sources: kolo/hutao reference idle PNGs, ganyu/varesa generated chibi PNGs. Outputs: kolo-motion.png, hutao-motion.png, ganyu-motion.png, varesa-motion.png.

Use case: identity-preserve sprite animation sheet.
Produce ONE production sprite atlas for the EXACT character from the reference image. Keep all identity, hair, costume, accessories, cute two-head-tall chibi proportions and soft detailed painterly rendering. No redesign.
Layout: wide rectangular 2048 by 1024 canvas, exactly 4 columns and 2 rows of equal 512 by 512 invisible cells. EXACTLY 8 full-body sprites, one centered in each cell. Every sprite at same character scale and orientation, generous 12% margin in each cell, no clipped hair, hat, horns, feet or hands. Real transparent background throughout. No labels, text, grid lines, shadow, floor, wall or ladder.
TOP ROW: 4 sequential key poses of a natural looping RUN, all facing RIGHT in consistent near-side profile (slight three-quarter face to see one and part of the other eye). Athletic but cute run with short limbs and subtle forward lean, oppositional bent arm swing. Frame1 contact: near leg forward foot touches ground, far leg behind. Frame2 passing/up: near leg beneath body pushing, far knee swings forward raised. Frame3 opposite contact: far leg forward planted, near leg behind. Frame4 opposite passing/up: far leg under body pushing, near knee lifted forward. DISTINCT alternating limb silhouettes, natural joints and no extra limbs. Keep head scale and torso position steady; only slight vertical rise at passing poses. Hair reacts mildly to motion. Do NOT just rotate or stretch a standing image.
BOTTOM ROW: 4 sequential poses for looping climbing UP a vertical wall to the RIGHT, same consistent right-facing three-quarter profile. Torso upright near imaginary wall, both hands reaching toward the right and elbows bent, knees bent with feet toward right. Frame1 near hand high grabs, far hand at shoulder, far knee high and near leg extends down. Frame2 pull body up, near elbow bends, near knee lifting, far hand begins reach. Frame3 far hand high grabs, near hand lower, near knee high and far leg extends down. Frame4 far elbow pulls, far knee lifts, near hand begins reach to loop. Make hand/foot contacts and alternating reach/pull clearly readable. No jumping, no running pose on bottom row, no horizontal sideways climbing. Keep head position approximately fixed within all four climb cells.
This atlas will be split into cells and played in order; consistent identity, scale, stable body pivot, clean separation between cells are critical. Each row must form smooth cyclic motion with frame4 naturally leading back to frame1.
