# V2.0 原神角色风之翼身体动画素材

Mode: built-in `image_gen.imagegen`, transparent RGBA. Three new assets; existing idle and movement artwork remain unchanged. This is original chibi animation inspired by the game's gliding posture, not a frame-by-frame extraction or replica.

Reference supplied by parent task: [Official gliding challenge introduction](https://genshin.hoyoverse.com/en/news/detail/7154), [official demonstration GIF](https://webstatic.hoyoverse.com/upload/uploadstatic/contentweb/20201106/2020110617515535894.gif). Posture cues: arms spread sideways for balance, upper body slightly forward, legs loosely bent behind/below the body. The anticipatory deployment and soft landing are desktop-pet adaptation.

The three existing `*-chibi.png` files are the authoritative style, costume, face and color references. Wings remain a separate layer; these sheets contain bodies only.

## hutao

Use case: stylized-concept.
Asset type: transparent production sprite atlas for a desktop pet, exactly FOUR COLUMNS and TWO ROWS, eight full-body animation frames, landscape 2:1.
The reference image is the authoritative character identity, rounded detailed chibi art style, pastel material colors, face and outfit. Create NEW full-body poses of this exact character in the same style. All eight frames share the same head diameter, short body proportions, costume, camera angle, face and shoulder/back pivot. Face right in a gentle three-quarter view with both eyes visible. Approximately two heads tall. Keep heads and bodies at fixed scale; only limbs and slight torso lean animate.
Primary request: eight sequential body animation poses for deploying a Genshin-style wind glider, gliding, and landing. WINGS ARE SEPARATE SOFTWARE LAYER: DO NOT DRAW ANY WINGS, feathers, harness, glider equipment, or effects. Draw the character body ONLY.
Pose order left-to-right across top row then bottom row:
1: airborne anticipation, elbows bend and hands lift diagonally outward near shoulders, knees softly bend and tiny feet begin trailing behind body.
2: deployment, both arms extend diagonally outward and slightly upward, torso inclines gently forward toward right, knees tuck just a little backward; balanced, cute, natural.
3: wings would finish opening; both arms settle outward around shoulder height, elbows slightly soft; body leans forward and both feet hang behind and below pelvis.
4: stable glide pose A, arms stretch sideways for balance, not gripping anything; slight forward body lean; tiny legs naturally bend back/down, with feet a little apart.
5: stable glide pose B, same head and shoulder position, one balancing arm gently rises a little while other lowers, feet relax and bend subtly in slipstream; calm face.
6: stable glide pose C, subtle opposite balance adjustment, maintaining gliding silhouette and naturally trailing bent legs. Not running and not flapping arms.
7: landing approach / wings retracting, arms lower toward waist, torso returns more upright, both short legs extend down to find ground.
8: soft landing with knees slightly flexed, feet near together beneath the body, lowered arms relaxed slightly outward for balance. No surface drawn.
CRITICAL: Frames 1-6 are AIRBORNE GLIDING poses, NOT eight standing T-poses. Arms must visibly lift, open, then lower across sequence. Full complete arms/hands and legs/feet in every frame. Torso leans mildly forward, never lying horizontal like Superman. Natural innocent fully clothed mascot.
Layout: exactly 4 evenly spaced equal cells per row and 2 rows. Identical scale and head diameter throughout, not fitted individually to cells. Each character fully contained inside its own cell with at least 10 percent fully transparent margin around every limb, hat/horns, hair and shoes. Ample completely empty gutters, no overlap with neighboring cell. Fixed head top/shoulder height across airborne poses; knees/feet change height naturally.
Output: genuinely transparent RGBA, no background, no checkerboard painted, no floor, no text/numbering, no grid, no border, no white outline, no shadow, no glow, no particles. Preserve soft low-saturation shading and fine clean edges from reference.

Character-specific invariants: Hu Tao: exactly the reference's short dark brown flat-topped hat with plum blossoms and gold paper emblem, brown hair with muted auburn tips, red blossom eyes, dark coat with antique gold embroidery and red details, dark shorts, tiny bare knees, dark socks and shoes. Do not enlarge or tallen hat. Preserve elegant black/brown and deep red colors; no butterfly transformation in this sheet.

## V2.0 final assembly

`glide-v20-crops.json` records all 24 crop rectangles, measured shoulder pivots and placements. `build_assets.py` reads this recipe directly. One scale per character is used for all eight poses: Hu Tao 0.86, Ganyu 0.90, Varesa 0.88. Airborne shoulders attach at (256,340) on the 512×512 canvas. Frames 7/8 shift vertically to put the feet at y=486: Hu Tao −16/−10, Ganyu −17/−13, Varesa −24/−20. The retracting wing roots follow the seventh-frame shift; wings are hidden for the eighth frame.

The runtime uses 0.56 seconds for deployment, a 4→5→4→6 glide cycle at 0.24 seconds per pose, up to 0.24 seconds to retract, and 0.20 seconds to settle on landing. Both wing halves and body share one clock. No recoloring, synthetic body drawing or per-pose scale changes were applied during packing. The final body resources are `{character}-glide-1.png` through `-8.png` inside `assets/characters.zip`.

Real WPF render previews: `release/V2.0/previews/wings-preview.gif` and `wings-contact.png`. Tests render 120 samples over 4.8 seconds; GIF encoding combines consecutive identical images while preserving the total duration.
